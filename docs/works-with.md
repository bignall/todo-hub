# Works with your other plugins

These plugins add tasks to To-do Hub when something needs you. They tick the tasks off themselves once it's sorted. On a connected site, their tasks go to the hub.

## Pulse Question

- **Add questions: N days left**, when the queue is getting short. It's high priority when two days or fewer are left, or when old questions are being asked again.
- **Approve N questions**, while questions are waiting for approval.
- **Review N held answers**, while answers are waiting for a moderator.
- **Daily email failed** or **Social post failed**, with the reason, until it works.

Turn them off under **Questions → Settings → To-do list**.

## Where to Buy

- **Fix store links on *item***, when a published item's store links don't all show. It's high priority when no button shows at all.
- **Review N items waiting for approval**, while items are pending.

Turn them off under **Where to Buy → General Settings → To-do list**.

## Ad Network

- **Fix ad *title***, for published ads that won't show, or whose cloaked link is gone or no longer pays. It's high priority when the offer no longer pays.
- **Add ads for N empty ad slots**, when a placement that's turned on has no ad of its size.

Turn them off under **Ads → Settings → To-do list**.

## Your own plugins

Any plugin can add tasks the same way. See [Developers](developers.md).
