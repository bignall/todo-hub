One to-do list for all your sites.

To-do Hub keeps your tasks in one place and puts what needs doing first at the top. Your plugins add tasks when something needs you, and tick them off when it's sorted. Your other sites send theirs too, and dated tasks show up on your Google Calendar.

The list sorts itself

You decide what matters; To-do Hub keeps it in order as days go by.

  1. Overdue and today first

    Anything overdue is at the top in red, then what's due today. A task with a time counts as overdue once that time has passed.

  2. Then by priority

    High, normal and low. Tasks move up as their due date gets close: within three days they count as two levels higher, within a week as one.

  3. Repeats take care of themselves

    Daily, weekly, monthly or every few days, weeks, months or years. Tick one off and the next is added, never already overdue.

  4. Plugins keep it honest

    Plugins add tasks like "Approve 6 questions" and tick them off once you've done it, so the list only shows what really needs you.

What's in it

  • Quick add

    Type a task and press Add, with a category, priority, date, time and repeat if you like.

  • Views

    Up next, By category, Today and Done, filtered by category or by the plugin or site that added a task.

  • One list, many sites

    One site keeps the list; your other sites send their tasks to it, and hear back when they're done.

  • Google Calendar

    Dated tasks become events on a "To-do" calendar, kept up to date, with done tasks ticked.

  • A badge and a widget

    The menu shows how many tasks are due now, and the dashboard shows what's next.

  • For developers

    A few PHP functions or actions, completion hooks, and a REST API for scripts and other apps.
